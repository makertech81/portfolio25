Built and published SetPy, a Python library for sorting, manipulating, and extracting statistics from time-windowed datasets — packaged and distributed on PyPI under the MIT license for public use.

While the core functionality is intentionally focused, the project reflects an early interest in building and shipping real, installable software rather than one-off scripts — from packaging and documentation to public distribution.
